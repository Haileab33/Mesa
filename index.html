About Mesa

A Taste of Ethiopia
In Every Bite

Mesa is not just a restaurant — it is a living tribute to the soul of Ethiopia. Founded with a deep respect for tradition, we bring the warmth, communal spirit, and rich flavors of Ethiopian culture to your table.

Every dish at Mesa is prepared using ancestral recipes, hand-ground spices, and the finest ingredients. Our injera is fermented the traditional way. Our wots simmer slowly, just as they did in kitchens across Addis Ababa and beyond.

We believe that food is the language of love, and we invite you to experience that love through every shared meal, every shared story, and every sip of our traditional Ethiopian coffee ceremony.

Our Story

The History
of Mesa

From a humble kitchen rooted in Ethiopian tradition to a celebrated destination for authentic East African cuisine — Mesa's journey is one of passion, family, and unwavering pride in culture.


Join Our Story
09

2009 — The Dream Begins

Mesa was born from the vision of founder Tigist Haile, who arrived from Addis Ababa with her grandmother's recipe book, a dream, and a deep love for Ethiopian culture. She opened a small 12-seat eatery, cooking every dish herself.

12

2012 — Recognition & Growth

Word spread fast. Mesa was featured in local food guides as a "hidden gem of Ethiopian cuisine." The growing demand pushed Tigist to expand her space and bring in family members to help carry the authentic vision forward.

15

2015 — The Coffee Ceremony

Mesa introduced the traditional Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony — a full 45-minute ritual of roasting, grinding, and brewing buna. This experience became our most iconic offering, drawing guests from around the world.

18

2018 — Award-Winning Kitchen

Mesa received the "Best Cultural Dining Experience" award. Our head chef, Chef Dawit Bekele, was honored for preserving traditional Ethiopian cooking techniques while elevating presentation for modern diners.

21

2021 — Resilience & Rebirth

Like many, we faced the challenges of the global pandemic. But Mesa's community rallied. Through community boxes, virtual cooking classes, and takeaway bundles, we kept the Mesa flame alive and stronger than ever.

25

2025 — A New Chapter

Today, Mesa stands as a cornerstone of Ethiopian culture in our city. With a renovated dining hall, an expanded menu, and a dedicated team of 30+ family members, we continue to serve with pride, love, and tradition.
